A straightforward commissioning process

  1. Clarify the need. Workforce, challenge, desired outcome and operational constraints.
  2. Agree the model. 1:1 support, clinics, workshops or a combined programme.
  3. Set boundaries. Confidentiality, safeguarding, referral routes and reporting are agreed before delivery.
  4. Deliver. Employees access support within the agreed model.
  5. Review. Appropriate anonymised utilisation and outcome information can inform next steps.

Reporting model

Organisations can receive aggregated or anonymised information such as utilisation, attendance, no-shows, broad non-identifying themes and aggregated outcome trends where appropriate.

Individual session notes and confidential disclosures are not provided to the employer.

Independent support can reduce the barrier to speaking openly.

Employees may not want to discuss every emotional or personal difficulty inside their management chain. Independent support provides a separate professional space within agreed safeguarding and governance boundaries.
