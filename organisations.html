The process

Five clear stages.

01

Clarify the need

Workforce, challenge, desired outcome and operational constraints.

02

Agree the model

1:1 support, clinics, workshops or a combined programme.

03

Set boundaries

Confidentiality, safeguarding, referral routes and reporting are agreed before delivery.

04

Deliver support

Employees access the agreed provision through a clear and manageable route.

05

Review

Appropriate anonymised utilisation and outcome information can inform next steps.

06

Adapt if needed

The model can evolve as organisational needs and workforce demand become clearer.

Confidentiality & reporting

Useful organisational visibility without exposing private employee conversations.

Employees may not want to discuss every emotional or personal difficulty inside their management chain. Independent support creates a separate professional space while still operating within agreed safeguarding and governance boundaries.

✓

Appropriate reporting can include
Utilisation, attendance, no-shows and broad non-identifying themes.

×

Reporting does not include
Individual session notes or confidential disclosures to the employer.
