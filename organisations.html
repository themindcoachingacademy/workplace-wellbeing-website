Why wait until people are overwhelmed to offer support?

Clearer Mind is a practical early-intervention wellbeing programme. We work with organisations to help adults recognise warning signs, interrupt unhelpful emotional and thinking patterns, and practise new responses before difficulties escalate.

The commissioning case

Offer practical skills before problems grow.

Community members may be experiencing low motivation, low confidence, anxiety, stress, emotional overwhelm, isolation, avoidance or recurring negative thinking. A non-clinical workshop creates an additional opportunity for early learning alongside existing services.

01

Earlier recognition

Help participants notice recurring patterns and signs that their wellbeing is being affected.

02

Practical participation

Interactive activities make ideas understandable and give participants techniques to practise outside sessions.

03

Complementary provision

Offer non-clinical education that sits alongside local referral, community and specialist pathways.

Who commissions Clearer Mind?

Different organisations. A shared opportunity to offer earlier support.

The value of a workshop depends on who you support and what you want to achieve. We tailor the discussion to your setting rather than offering the same pitch to everyone.

01

Councils & community funders

Provide accessible, practical learning for residents facing low confidence, isolation, emotional overwhelm or recurring patterns. Discuss targeted neighbourhood delivery and funded community programmes.

02

Housing associations & charities

Offer tenants, service users or community groups an additional, non-clinical route to build understanding, coping skills and confidence, alongside existing support and referral pathways.

03

Employers & businesses

Give employees practical opportunities to recognise pressure, understand unhelpful reactions, build confidence and practise emotional self-management. Workshops can complement established staff wellbeing initiatives.

What you're commissioning

More than a presentation about wellbeing.

Clearer Mind is designed as a participatory learning experience. Sessions can include accessible explanations, guided reflection, practical demonstrations, voluntary exercises and ideas participants can try in daily life.

We agree the intended audience, suitable level of support, venue or delivery arrangements, accessibility, participant recruitment and feedback approach before work begins. We do not promise clinical outcomes or guaranteed reductions in absence, referrals or service demand.
