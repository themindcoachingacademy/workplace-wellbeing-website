Why wait until people are overwhelmed to offer support?

Clearer Mind is a practical early-intervention wellbeing programme. We work with organisations to help adults recognise warning signs, interrupt unhelpful emotional and thinking patterns, and practise new responses before difficulties escalate.

The commissioning case

Offer practical skills before problems grow.

Community members may be experiencing low motivation, low confidence, anxiety, stress, emotional overwhelm, isolation, avoidance or recurring negative thinking. A non-clinical workshop creates an additional opportunity for early learning alongside existing services.

01

Earlier recognition

Help participants notice recurring patterns and signs that their wellbeing is being affected.

02

Practical participation

Interactive activities make ideas understandable and give participants techniques to practise outside sessions.

03

Complementary provision

Offer non-clinical education that sits alongside local referral, community and specialist pathways.

Built around local needs

Commission a format that fits your community.

Our introduction workshop, three-session series and full six-session Clearer Mind programme provide different levels of engagement. We can discuss one-off pilots, regular delivery, annual programmes and multi-site partnerships.

Projects are planned around audience, accessibility, recruitment, venue arrangements and appropriate participant feedback. We do not publish fixed prices because delivery requirements vary.
