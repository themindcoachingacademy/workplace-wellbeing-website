What an employer gains

When people are functioning better, the organisation benefits too.

The objective is not to promise that one wellbeing service will magically remove absence or guarantee productivity. It is to improve the conditions that help employees function more effectively: earlier support, better coping, clearer thinking, stronger confidence and a professional route when pressure is affecting work.

Protect concentration and decision-makingMental overload, worry and poor emotional regulation can consume attention and reduce the capacity available for the job.
Reduce the impact of presenteeismEmployees can be physically present but operating below their normal level. Early support gives them somewhere to address what is affecting their functioning.
Support attendance and earlier return to stabilityGiving people a route into help before a difficulty becomes more entrenched can support remaining in work or returning to normal functioning sooner.
Reduce pressure on managersLine managers can listen, support and signpost without becoming the employee's counsellor or carrying problems they are not trained to resolve.
Strengthen engagement and retentionVisible, credible support contributes to an employee experience in which people feel the organisation takes their wellbeing seriously.
Protect team performanceWhen one employee is struggling, workload, communication and emotional pressure can spread across the team. Supporting the individual can reduce that wider operational drag.
1:1 support is for the individual employee whose stress, anxiety, confidence or personal circumstances are affecting how they feel and function.
