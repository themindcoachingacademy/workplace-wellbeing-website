The gap in workplace support

Employees do not have to be off sick before they need help.

People can remain at work while struggling with persistent worry, emotional pressure, difficult life events, reduced confidence or an inability to switch off. Left unsupported, those difficulties can affect concentration, relationships, performance, engagement and attendance.

Early support

Give employees somewhere to address difficulties before they become more disruptive.

A genuinely independent space

Some employees find it easier to speak openly with someone outside their employer or management structure.

Change, not just conversation

Support is structured around understanding unhelpful patterns and developing more effective responses.
