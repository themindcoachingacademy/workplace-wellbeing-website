The full 1:1 change toolkit

Different problems need different interventions.

The work is not limited to stress management or a single NLP exercise. Steven draws from NLP-based change work, life coaching, EFT and, where appropriate and agreed, guided hypnotic or relaxation methods. The intervention is selected around the employee’s actual pattern and the outcome they need.

01

Limiting beliefs

What we work on: beliefs such as “I’m not good enough”, “I’ll get this wrong” or “I can’t cope”. We examine how the belief is being reinforced, challenge unhelpful assumptions and build a more useful frame.

Employer benefit: greater willingness to act, contribute, make decisions and take appropriate responsibility rather than withdrawing or repeatedly seeking reassurance.

02

Stress, anxiety & triggers

What we work on: the trigger, internal dialogue, mental imagery, physical response and automatic behaviour. Techniques can include reframing, anchoring, state management and pattern interruption.

Employer benefit: employees who are better able to remain composed, think clearly and respond constructively under pressure.

03

Confidence & self-belief

What we work on: where confidence drops, the comparisons or memories feeding it, and how the employee accesses more useful resource states.

Employer benefit: more confident communication, stronger participation, greater ownership and less performance drag from self-doubt.

04

Overthinking & rumination

What we work on: repetitive self-talk, imagined future scenarios and internal representations that keep attention locked on the problem. NLP-informed submodality work and pattern interruption may be used where appropriate.

Employer benefit: more attention available for tasks, decisions and conversations rather than being consumed by repetitive worry.

05

Emotional regulation & EFT

What we work on: emotionally charged responses that are difficult to settle. EFT and other regulation strategies may be used as part of a broader coaching plan where appropriate.

Employer benefit: fewer situations in which emotional overload spills into communication, concentration or working relationships.

06

Anchoring & state management

What we work on: helping the employee access calmer, more focused or more confident states before demanding situations such as meetings, presentations or difficult conversations.

Employer benefit: better preparation and more consistent behaviour when the stakes or pressure increase.

07

Reframing & perspective

What we work on: the meaning attached to feedback, conflict, mistakes, change or setbacks. Reframing helps create alternative interpretations that support more useful responses.

Employer benefit: less unnecessary escalation, more constructive problem-solving and greater adaptability.

08

Behaviour patterns & habits

What we work on: repeated responses such as avoidance, procrastination, shutting down, people-pleasing or reacting automatically under pressure. We identify the sequence and practise an alternative response.

Employer benefit: more reliable follow-through, stronger accountability and fewer recurring behavioural patterns that undermine performance.

09

Life coaching & goal clarity

What we work on: priorities, boundaries, decision-making, motivation, accountability and practical next steps when an employee feels stuck or directionless.

Employer benefit: clearer priorities, better ownership and more purposeful action rather than prolonged indecision.

10

Internal conflict & competing needs

What we work on: situations where part of the person wants one outcome while another part resists it — for example wanting promotion while fearing visibility or wanting boundaries while fearing disapproval.

Employer benefit: less stop-start behaviour and clearer decisions where internal conflict has been blocking action.

11

Future rehearsal & future pacing

What we work on: mentally rehearsing a more useful response to the real situation that has previously triggered stress, avoidance or low confidence.

Employer benefit: the work is translated into the workplace rather than remaining a useful conversation that never changes behaviour.

12

Guided relaxation & hypnotic methods

What we work on: where appropriate, consented methods can be used to help reduce excessive arousal, focus attention and support rehearsal of useful responses. These are not presented as medical treatment.

Employer benefit: another practical route for employees who need help settling mental noise or preparing for demanding situations.
