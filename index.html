How we create change in 1:1 support

We find what is driving the problem, change the pattern and practise a better response.

Stress, anxiety and low confidence are not identical experiences. We look at what the employee is thinking, believing, imagining and doing when the difficulty occurs, then choose the intervention around that individual pattern.

01

Limiting beliefs

If somebody is operating from beliefs such as “I’m not good enough”, “I’ll fail” or “I can’t cope”, we examine and challenge the assumptions supporting that belief, reframe its meaning and help build a more useful belief that supports different behaviour.

02

Stress, anxiety & triggers

We map the trigger, self-talk, mental images, emotional state and automatic response. NLP-informed techniques can include reframing, anchoring, state management, changing internal representations, pattern interruption and future rehearsal.

03

Confidence & performance

We identify where confidence drops, reconnect the employee with useful resource states, challenge unhelpful comparisons or beliefs and rehearse handling the real workplace situation with a stronger response.
