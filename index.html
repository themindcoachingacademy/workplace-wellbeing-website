The Clearer Mind Project

Recognising emotional difficulties early, before they spiral into something bigger.

Stress, anxiety, overthinking and emotional overwhelm don't always appear overnight. They can build gradually, affecting how we think, feel and respond. Clearer Mind helps people recognise these patterns early, interrupt the cycle and develop practical skills to create positive change before difficulties become more deeply established.

Why Clearer Mind exists

Support shouldn't have to wait until someone reaches breaking point.

Stress, overthinking, anxiety, emotional overload and low confidence can build gradually, influencing how people feel, communicate and respond to everyday challenges.

Clearer Mind focuses on helping people recognise difficulties earlier and develop practical ways of responding. Our interactive programmes complement existing wellbeing and specialist services; they do not replace clinical care.

What makes Clearer Mind different

The Clearer Mind Method

More than awareness: an adaptable framework to understand unhelpful responses and develop practical alternatives. Not every person needs every stage in the same way.

01

NOTICE

Notice triggers, beliefs, thoughts and automatic emotional responses that may contribute to a difficulty.

02

INTERRUPT

Break an unhelpful automatic cycle and create an opportunity to respond differently.

03

REFRAME

Where helpful, explore interpretations, assumptions and alternative perspectives to develop a more useful response.

04

APPLY

Use practical tools and new responses in real situations, beyond the workshop or coaching session.

Why early intervention matters

People should be able to find support before problems escalate.

Difficulties with stress, anxiety, emotional reactions and confidence can affect everyday life long before someone seeks specialist help.

Practical support, at an earlier stage.

Clearer Mind creates accessible opportunities to recognise unhelpful patterns, learn techniques for interrupting them and develop more useful ways of responding.

The programme complements existing services, with appropriate professional boundaries and signposting when someone's needs fall outside our non-clinical scope.

What delivery feels like

Practical sessions, not another presentation.

Clearer Mind is designed to be approachable and interactive. Participants are given opportunities to understand common patterns, try relevant techniques and consider where the learning could be useful in everyday life.

01

Understand the challenge

Clear, relatable explanations of how thoughts, emotions and automatic reactions can become habitual.

02

Explore a different response

Short, guided exercises to notice triggers, interrupt familiar patterns and consider other responses.

03

Take something usable away

Practical tools to consider using after the session, without pressure to share private experiences.

Clearer Mind is non-clinical wellbeing support. It complements, rather than replaces, specialist healthcare or organisational responsibilities.

What participants will take away

Clearer Mind is designed to give participants more than an enjoyable workshop. The aim is to help them understand their own thinking and emotional patterns, discover different ways of responding to difficult situations and leave with practical skills they can use in everyday life.

  • Greater self-awareness: Recognise early warning signs, emotional triggers and unhelpful thinking patterns before they escalate.
  • A different perspective: Learn to challenge limiting beliefs, question automatic assumptions and look at situations in more helpful ways.
  • Practical emotional-regulation techniques: Develop ways to interrupt overthinking, manage emotional reactions and respond more calmly.
  • Confidence to make changes: Practise new approaches that can help participants feel more capable of handling everyday challenges.
  • A workbook to take home: Written exercises, practical techniques and useful reminders to reinforce learning and continue practising after the workshop.

Sessions are relaxed and interactive, with no pressure to share personal experiences.

The practitioner behind Clearer Mind

Meet the practitioner behind Clearer Mind.

Real experience. Practical change work. Direct delivery.

I'm Steven Rooney, Director of The Mind Coaching Academy CIC. For more than 12 years, I've worked with adults who want to manage emotional pressures, build confidence and change patterns of thinking and behaviour that keep getting in their way.

I'm an NLP Master Practitioner and Life Coach, with additional training in EFT and behaviour change. I've delivered emotional-regulation and wellbeing workshops involving NHS staff at Whiston Hospital, alongside more than a decade of individual coaching work. That experience informs the practical, non-clinical approach behind Clearer Mind.

Clearer Mind brings that experience into accessible, non-clinical early-intervention programmes for both communities and workplaces. I work directly with partners to understand the people they support, agree a suitable format and deliver practical sessions that encourage meaningful participation without requiring personal disclosure.
