What you gain as the employer

The value is not “wellbeing” in the abstract. It is a workforce that can function more effectively.

The service is designed to help employees address mental and emotional pressures earlier, so they are better placed to think clearly, communicate, make decisions, cope with challenge and remain engaged with work. We do not promise guaranteed reductions in absence or a fixed ROI; we focus on the areas an employer can reasonably influence through earlier, accessible support.

Protect concentration and judgementPersistent worry and emotional overload can consume attention that would otherwise be available for the job.
Reduce presenteeism dragEmployees can be physically present but operating below their normal level. Support gives them somewhere to address what is affecting their functioning.
Support attendance and stabilityEarlier access creates an opportunity to address difficulties before they become more entrenched or harder to manage.
Reduce pressure on managersManagers can listen, support and signpost without becoming responsible for complex emotional support outside their role.
Strengthen engagement and retentionVisible, credible support contributes to an employee experience in which people feel their wellbeing is taken seriously.
Protect team capacityWhen one employee is struggling, workload and pressure often spread to colleagues. Earlier support can reduce that wider operational drag.
1:1 support is for the employee whose stress, anxiety, confidence or personal circumstances are affecting how they feel and function.
