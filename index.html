Why early support matters

People can be struggling long before they are absent.

Persistent worry, difficult circumstances, emotional overload or a loss of confidence can affect concentration, communication, motivation and attendance while somebody is still showing up for work.

Providing an independent route into support gives employees somewhere to address what is happening earlier, without replacing the services already available inside the organisation.
