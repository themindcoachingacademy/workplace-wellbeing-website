Why earlier action matters

Pressure often shows up before someone takes time off.

An employee might still be attending work while struggling with overthinking, low confidence, irritability or emotional overload. Their experience may affect concentration, communication and how they respond to everyday challenges.

Our non-clinical workshops and coaching give people opportunities to recognise those patterns and practise more useful responses. They complement — and never replace — an employer's responsibility to identify and reduce organisational causes of work-related stress.
