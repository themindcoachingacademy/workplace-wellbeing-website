Why early support matters

Employees do not have to be off sick before they need help.

People can remain at work while carrying significant emotional pressure. Persistent worry, difficult personal circumstances, low confidence or an inability to switch off can affect concentration, communication, motivation and attendance long before somebody reaches crisis point.

Providing a confidential route into support gives employees somewhere to address what is happening earlier, while helping the organisation add an independent layer alongside existing HR, Occupational Health or EAP provision.

The service is designed to complement — not replace
  • HR and People teams
  • Occupational Health
  • Employee Assistance Programmes
  • Clinical and emergency mental-health pathways
