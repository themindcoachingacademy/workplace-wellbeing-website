Where the service can fit

Different organisations need different access models.

The same core principles apply — independent, confidential, practical support — while the structure can be adapted to the environment.

Independent support that can sit alongside People, HR, EAP or internal wellbeing provision.

02

SMEs

Flexible access to employee support without the need to create a large internal wellbeing infrastructure.

03

Support for employees working in demanding environments where emotional pressure can accumulate.

04

Independent early-intervention support designed to complement internal wellbeing, Occupational Health and EAP routes.

05

Practical wellbeing support that can be commissioned around workforce needs and governance requirements.
