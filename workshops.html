Workshops can be delivered as standalone sessions or as part of a wider wellbeing programme. Content, duration, group size and delivery format can be agreed around the workforce and the outcomes the organisation wants to support.

On-site

Interactive sessions delivered within the workplace where appropriate.

Remote

Online delivery for distributed teams and organisations requiring greater flexibility.

Programme-based

Multiple sessions can be combined with individual employee support as part of a broader intervention.
