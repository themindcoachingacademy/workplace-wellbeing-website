Why an employer would commission workshops

One-to-one support helps the individual. Workshops help the wider workforce build capability.

The workshop offer is deliberately different from confidential 1:1 support. Its purpose is to reach groups, normalise useful conversations, help employees recognise patterns earlier and give them tools they can use before stress or worry becomes more disruptive.

Prevention at scaleReach more people before they require individual intervention.
Shared languageHelp teams understand stress, overthinking and emotional pressure using practical, non-clinical language.
Practical self-managementEmployees leave with strategies they can apply after the session rather than just awareness.
Visible investment in wellbeingDemonstrate that the organisation is providing active support rather than relying only on policies or signposting.

When workshops are the right choice

Use workshops when the need is broad, shared or preventative.

If one employee needs private individual support, use the 1:1 service. If many employees are dealing with similar pressures or the organisation wants to build preventative capability, workshops are the stronger route.
