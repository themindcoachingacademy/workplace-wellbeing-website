Why an employer would commission workshops

One-to-one support helps the individual. Workshops build capability across a wider group.

The workshop offer is deliberately different from confidential 1:1 support. Its purpose is to reach groups, improve recognition of unhelpful patterns and give employees practical ways to respond before pressure becomes more disruptive.

Prevention at scaleReach more employees before they require individual intervention.
Shared languageHelp teams understand stress, overthinking and emotional pressure using practical, non-clinical language.
Practical self-managementEmployees leave with strategies they can apply after the session rather than awareness alone.
Earlier recognitionEmployees are better placed to notice when pressure is building and know when further support may be appropriate.

Workshops sit inside a wider prevention strategy

HSE identifies six areas of work design that can contribute to work-related stress.

Source: Health and Safety Executive — work-related stress.
01
Demands

Workload, work patterns and the working environment.

02
Control

How much say people have in the way they do their work.

03
Support

The resources, encouragement and help available from the employer and colleagues.

04
Relationships, role & change

The remaining HSE areas cover working relationships, role clarity and how organisational change is managed.

HSE states that employers have a legal duty to protect workers from stress at work through risk assessment and action. Workshops can support awareness and individual capability, but they do not replace the employer's responsibility to identify and manage organisational causes of work-related stress.
The objective is application: employees should leave knowing what they can do differently when pressure shows up.

When workshops are the right choice

Use workshops when the need is broad, shared or preventative.

If one employee needs private individual support, use the 1:1 service. If many employees are dealing with similar pressures or the employer wants to build preventative capability, workshops are the stronger route.

Broad workforce need
A shared challenge such as stress, anxiety or resilience.
