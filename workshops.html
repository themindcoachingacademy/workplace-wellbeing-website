Tailored delivery

Built around the workforce rather than delivered as a generic wellbeing lecture.

Workshops can be delivered as standalone sessions or as part of a wider wellbeing programme. Content, duration, group size and delivery format can be agreed around the outcomes the organisation wants to support.

Delivery options
  • On-site interactive workshops
  • Remote sessions for distributed teams
  • Single-topic standalone sessions
  • Multi-session programmes
  • Combined workshop and 1:1 support models
Workshops are designed to feel relevant to real working lives rather than theoretical wellbeing education.
