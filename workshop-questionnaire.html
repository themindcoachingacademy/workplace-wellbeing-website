Employee feedback · 2–3 minutes · Voluntary

Which practical workplace skills would help you most?

We are exploring live, interactive wellbeing workshops. Please do not include your name, medical details, personal experiences or information about colleagues. You may skip any question. Your employer should explain how completed responses will be handled.

1. Which skills would you most value practising at work? (Choose up to three.)
2. How useful would a practical group workshop be for you?
3. Which activities would help you learn best? (Choose up to two.)
4. What would make a workshop easier to attend?

Important for employers: This is a workshop topic-planning aid, not a medical questionnaire, diagnosis, mental health screening or HSE-compliant stress risk assessment. Participation must be voluntary. Do not ask for names; summarise results only where groups are large enough to protect privacy. Consider existing employee consultation and work-related stress assessments as well.
