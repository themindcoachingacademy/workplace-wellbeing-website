What happens next

A straightforward commissioning conversation.

01

Clarify the problem

What is happening in the workforce, who is affected and what would a better outcome look like?

02

Identify the right route

1:1 support, on-site clinic, workshop or a combined model based on the actual need.

03

Test the practicalities

Capacity, location, access, confidentiality, safeguarding, reporting and any governance requirements.

No pressure to fit a package

The objective of the first discussion is to understand the requirement and establish whether this service can meet it responsibly — not to force every organisation into the same offer.
