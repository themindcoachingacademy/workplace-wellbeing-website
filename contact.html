Contact

Start with a straightforward conversation.

Email the.mind.coaching.academy@gmail.com with your organisation, location, intended audience and the type of support you're considering.

We are based in St Helens and currently focused on delivery across the North West, while welcoming discussions about future partnerships elsewhere in the UK.

Clearer Mind is not a crisis or emergency service. For immediate danger call 999; for urgent mental health support in England call NHS 111 and select the mental health option.
