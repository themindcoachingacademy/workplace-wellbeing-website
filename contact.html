Contact

Start with a straightforward conversation.

Email the.mind.coaching.academy@gmail.com with your organisation, location, who you want to support, the difficulties you are seeing and whether you have an existing budget or are exploring funding. You do not need a fully developed brief to start a conversation.

We are based in St Helens and currently focused on delivery across the North West, while welcoming discussions about future partnerships elsewhere in the UK.

Clearer Mind is not a crisis or emergency service. For immediate danger call 999; for urgent mental health support in England call NHS 111 and select the mental health option.

What happens next

We'll explore the right fit.

We can discuss your organisation's needs, the people you support, suitable workshop formats, practical arrangements, budget and realistic next steps without assuming a fixed package.
