What happens next

A clear scoping conversation — not a generic sales pitch.

01Understand the problem

What is happening, who is affected and how is it showing up in attendance, performance, confidence, morale, access or manager time?

02Identify the right route

1:1 support, on-site clinics, workshops or a defined combination.

03Agree the operating model

Capacity, access, confidentiality, safeguarding, reporting, timescale and review expectations.

Helpful information — if you have it
  • Employer name and sector
  • Approximate workforce or team size
  • The issue you want to reduce, improve or prevent
  • What support employees can already access
  • Location, timescale or procurement requirements

You do not need all of this before making contact.

Why independence can matter

For some employees, a practitioner who sits outside line management, HR and formal workplace processes may provide an additional route in which they feel able to speak openly.

That independence is paired with explicit safeguarding, scope and confidentiality boundaries.
