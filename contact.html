What to include

Workforce size, location, the problem you want to improve and any support you already have. That is enough to begin.

What happens after you make contact

A clear employer conversation, not a generic sales call.

01Understand the workforce problem

What is happening, who is affected and how is it showing up in attendance, performance, confidence, morale or manager time?

02Identify the right intervention

1:1 support, on-site clinics, workshops or a combination based on the actual need.

03Agree the practicalities

Access, capacity, location, confidentiality, safeguarding, reporting and review expectations.

Useful details to send
  • Employer name and sector
  • Approximate workforce or team size
  • The issue you want to reduce, improve or prevent
  • What support employees can already access
  • Location, timescale or procurement requirements

You do not need all five. A few lines about the problem are enough to start.

What you can expect

If the service is not the right fit, that should become clear early.

The purpose of the initial discussion is to understand the requirement and establish whether this service can meet it responsibly. The goal is not to force every employer into the same package.
