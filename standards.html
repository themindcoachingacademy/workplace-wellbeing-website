Professional Standards

Clear scope, confidentiality and responsible escalation.

The workplace service is for adults aged 18+ and provides non-clinical coaching and wellbeing support.

Confidentiality

Session content is confidential subject to safeguarding, serious-risk and legal exceptions.

Scope of Practice

The service does not replace medical diagnosis, psychotherapy, psychiatric care or emergency mental-health services.

Safeguarding

Where significant risk or safeguarding concerns arise, appropriate escalation may be necessary.

Record Keeping

Sessions are not routinely recorded. Brief, proportionate confidential notes are retained securely online with access restricted to the practitioner.

Organisational Reporting

Employers receive appropriate anonymised or aggregated programme information rather than confidential session content.

Complaints

Formal complaints are handled through a documented process, with a substantive response target of 10 working days.
