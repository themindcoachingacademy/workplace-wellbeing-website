About the practitioner-led service

Employers need to know who they are trusting with their people.

The workplace service is led by Steven Rooney and is built around clear professional boundaries, practical change work and an independent relationship with the employee.

Steven Rooney

A human service, not an anonymous wellbeing portal.

Steven works directly with people experiencing anxiety, emotional overwhelm, overthinking, reduced confidence and difficult life or workplace pressures.

His approach combines coaching, NLP-based change work and EFT techniques, with the emphasis on understanding the patterns influencing how a person thinks, feels and responds — then helping them create practical change.

For employers, the value of a practitioner-led service is accountability: there is a clear person responsible for delivery, boundaries, safeguarding and the employee experience.

What an employer should expect
  • A clearly defined non-clinical scope of practice
  • Confidentiality explained before support begins
  • Safeguarding and escalation boundaries
  • Professional, proportionate record keeping
  • Appropriate employer reporting without routine disclosure of private session content
The service is designed for adults in working environments and is focused on practical wellbeing support.
The working approach

Understand the person. Identify the pattern. Create useful change.

01Understand

What is happening, how it is affecting the employee and what they want to change.

02Map the pattern

Situations, thoughts, emotional responses and behaviours that may be maintaining the difficulty.

03Change the response

Use appropriate coaching and change-work techniques to build more useful ways of responding.

04Apply it

Translate the work into everyday situations, decisions and behaviour outside the session.

Professional boundaries

Independent does not mean unaccountable.

The service is non-clinical and complements appropriate employer and healthcare pathways. Where a person requires medical, psychiatric, emergency or specialist clinical support, the appropriate route should be used.
