About The Mind Coaching Academy CIC

Practical early intervention, before difficulties become overwhelming.

Clearer Mind is delivered by The Mind Coaching Academy CIC, a St Helens-based community interest company. We help people who are already experiencing emotional and everyday difficulties recognise unhelpful patterns, practise different responses and build skills they can use beyond the workshop.

Why Clearer Mind exists

People should not have to reach crisis point before learning useful skills.

Overthinking, low confidence, anxiety, emotional overload, avoidance, isolation and feeling stuck can already be affecting someone's life — even when they do not need, or are not accessing, clinical services.

Clearer Mind offers a practical educational space to notice what is happening, explore the connection between thoughts, feelings and actions, interrupt familiar patterns and practise alternative responses. Our aim is to support earlier understanding and action, not to diagnose or treat a condition.

Our approach

Recognise. Interrupt. Practise.

Structured, accessible workshops that combine straightforward explanations, guided reflection and practical activities. No participant is required to share personal experiences with the group.

Clear professional boundaries

Clearer Mind is non-clinical wellbeing education. It does not replace therapy, diagnosis, medical care or crisis services; appropriate signposting is part of responsible delivery.

What organisations can expect

A clear, accountable delivery partner.

Commissioners work directly with the person delivering the programme. Together we agree the audience, intended learning outcomes, suitable format, recruitment responsibilities, accessibility, safeguarding arrangements and proportionate participant feedback.

Our approach is designed to complement the work of councils, housing providers, charities, community organisations and employers — not duplicate specialist services.

Flexible commissioning

From an initial workshop to an ongoing partnership.

We can discuss introductory sessions, deeper learning programmes, repeat workshops and delivery across multiple groups or locations. Scope and costs are agreed before delivery.
