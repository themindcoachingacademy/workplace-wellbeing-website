Why practitioner accountability matters

Employers should know exactly who they are trusting with their people.

The workplace service is led directly by Steven Rooney. There is no anonymous portal between the employer, the practitioner and the standard of delivery: one accountable practitioner, clear boundaries and a defined support model.

Steven works directly with adults experiencing anxiety, emotional overwhelm, overthinking, reduced confidence and personal or workplace pressures that are affecting how they feel, cope and function.

The approach combines coaching, NLP-based change work and EFT techniques. The emphasis is practical: understand the pattern influencing thoughts, emotions and behaviour, then help the person create more useful responses they can apply outside the session.

For employers, practitioner-led delivery means accountability. There is a clear person responsible for the employee experience, confidentiality boundaries, safeguarding, records, service reporting and professional scope.

What an employer should expect
  • A clearly defined non-clinical scope
  • Confidentiality explained before support begins
  • Safeguarding and escalation boundaries
  • Professional, proportionate record keeping
  • Service-level reporting without routine disclosure of private content
  • Honest signposting when another service is more appropriate

The working approach

Understand. Map. Change. Apply.

01Understand

What is happening, how it is affecting the employee and what they want to change.

02Map the pattern

Situations, thoughts, emotional responses and behaviours that may be maintaining the difficulty.

03Change the response

Use appropriate coaching and change-work techniques to create more useful ways of responding.

04Apply it

Translate the work into everyday situations, decisions and behaviour outside the session.

What I will not claim
