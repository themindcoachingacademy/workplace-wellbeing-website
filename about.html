About The Mind Coaching Academy

Independent support focused on practical, meaningful change.

Workplace wellbeing support designed to sit alongside existing HR, Occupational Health, EAP and clinical pathways — not compete with them.

Steven Rooney

A practitioner-led service with a clear human focus.

Steven works with people experiencing anxiety, emotional overwhelm, overthinking, reduced confidence and difficult life or workplace pressures.

His approach combines coaching, NLP-based change work and EFT techniques, with the emphasis on helping people understand and change the patterns influencing how they think, feel and respond.

For organisations, the service is structured to provide an independent route into support while maintaining clear confidentiality, safeguarding and scope-of-practice boundaries.
