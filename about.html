About

Professional support focused on practical change.

The Mind Coaching Academy provides independent workplace wellbeing support designed to complement, not replace, existing HR, Occupational Health, EAP and clinical pathways.

Steven Rooney

Steven is the founder of The Mind Coaching Academy and works with people experiencing anxiety, emotional overwhelm, overthinking, reduced confidence and difficult life or workplace pressures.

His approach combines coaching, NLP-based change work and EFT techniques, with a focus on helping people understand and change the patterns influencing how they think, feel and respond.
