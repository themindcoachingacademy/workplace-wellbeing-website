Why I do this work

I help people change what is getting in the way of how they want to live, work and perform.

People do not usually arrive with one neat problem. Stress can affect confidence. Overthinking can affect decisions. A limiting belief can affect communication. A difficult experience can change how somebody responds under pressure.

My work is about understanding what is actually happening for that person, identifying the pattern that is keeping the difficulty in place and helping them create a more useful response they can use outside the session.

That same principle sits behind the workplace service: support the individual in a practical, confidential way while giving the employer a clear and accountable provision.

The context may change, but the question is often similar: what is happening internally that is preventing this person from responding or performing as they want to?
My approach

Understand the person before choosing the technique.

01Listen first

Understand the problem, its impact and what the person wants instead.

02Find the pattern

Identify the thoughts, beliefs, triggers, emotional responses or behaviours maintaining the difficulty.

03Choose the right intervention

Draw from NLP-informed change work, life coaching, EFT and other appropriate methods rather than forcing every person through the same process.

04Apply the change

Connect the work to the real situations where the person needs to think, feel, communicate or perform differently.

Workplace support grew from real conversations

Independent support matters when people need somewhere they can speak openly.

My workplace wellbeing work developed from seeing how mental and emotional pressures affect people before those difficulties necessarily become a formal absence or clinical issue. Conversations with people working in demanding environments, including NHS staff, reinforced the potential value of an independent route sitting outside management and HR.

Local NHS experience

I have worked one-to-one with Caroline Roberts from Whiston Hospital and discussed the pressures experienced by NHS staff. Caroline encouraged the development of an independent NHS wellbeing pilot and has given permission for that support to be referenced.

This is a personal endorsement of the concept and not an official endorsement by Whiston Hospital or an NHS Trust.
