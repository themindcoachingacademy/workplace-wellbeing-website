1:1 Employee Support

Independent support when an employee is struggling.

Confidential, practical one-to-one support for employees experiencing stress, anxiety, emotional overwhelm, overthinking, reduced confidence or difficult personal circumstances that are affecting them at work.

Early intervention

Support does not have to wait until someone reaches crisis point.

Employees can remain at work while carrying significant emotional pressure. Early access to an independent practitioner gives them an opportunity to address what is happening, understand the patterns maintaining the difficulty and develop more effective responses before the problem becomes more disruptive.

A private space

An employee can speak openly with someone who is separate from their management structure.

Practical change

Sessions are designed to help the individual understand and change unhelpful thought, emotional and behavioural patterns.

Flexible delivery

Support can be delivered remotely by Google Meet and, where agreed, face to face.

How it works

A straightforward route into support.

1. Organisation provides access

The organisation agrees the support arrangement and how eligible employees will access it.

2. Employee books directly

Where self-booking is selected, the employee can choose an available appointment without having to arrange each session through management.

3. Support remains individual

The work focuses on the employee's needs while maintaining agreed confidentiality and safeguarding boundaries.

Booking structure

Designed to keep access manageable.

Employees can book one appointment at a time, with a maximum of one appointment per week. The organisation can decide the overall level of access available to its workforce and the appropriate delivery arrangement.

For employers

Add an independent layer of support alongside your existing wellbeing provision.

Employee support can complement HR, occupational health and EAP provision rather than attempting to replace it.
