1:1 employee support

Independent support when an employee is carrying more than they can manage alone.

Confidential, practical one-to-one support for employees experiencing stress, anxiety, emotional overwhelm, overthinking, reduced confidence or difficult circumstances affecting them at work.

Discuss Employee Support
Early intervention

Support does not have to wait until somebody reaches crisis point.

Employees can remain at work while carrying significant emotional pressure. Early access to an independent practitioner gives them a private place to understand what is happening and develop more effective responses before the difficulty becomes more disruptive.

Support can focus on
  • Stress and persistent worry
  • Anxiety and emotional overwhelm
  • Overthinking and difficulty switching off
  • Confidence and resilience
  • Pressure inside or outside work
  • Practical strategies for moving forward
How it works

A straightforward route into confidential support.

01

Organisation provides access

The organisation agrees the support arrangement, eligibility and overall access model.

02

Employee books directly

Where self-booking is selected, employees choose an available appointment without arranging each session through management.

03

Support remains individual

The work focuses on the employee’s needs within agreed confidentiality, safeguarding and professional boundaries.

Delivery

Flexible access without losing structure.

Remote sessions are available by Google Meet, with face-to-face support where agreed. Employees can book one appointment at a time, with a maximum of one appointment per week.
