ABOUT STEVEN

The personal experience behind Clearer Mind.

I'm Steven Rooney, Director of The Mind Coaching Academy CIC. Clearer Mind grew out of my own experience of struggling and my belief that people deserve practical support before difficulties become overwhelming.

Why this matters to me

I know what it feels like to get stuck.

I've personally experienced severe depression, anxiety, isolation and suicidal thoughts. There were times when I withdrew from people and activities that mattered to me, stopped enjoying things I once valued and became caught in destructive patterns of thinking and behaviour. I know how overwhelming and lonely those experiences can feel.

Discovering practical change-work approaches, including NLP, helped me begin looking at those experiences differently and responding in new ways. That did not give me a reason to claim one technique works for everyone. It did give me a powerful reason to make practical skills more accessible to others.

From experience to purpose

Why I created Clearer Mind

I often think about how valuable it would have been to recognise my own warning signs earlier, interrupt familiar reactions and know what else I could try. I cannot say early support would have prevented my difficulties. But that question has stayed with me.

It's why Clearer Mind is built around practical early intervention. People should have opportunities to learn skills for managing everyday challenges without first having to reach crisis point. No one is asked to share private experiences in a group, and the programme is not a replacement for specialist mental health care.

Experience and delivery

Working directly with the person behind the programme

I have more than 12 years' experience coaching adults and am an NLP Master Practitioner, Life Coach and EFT Practitioner. My work has included emotional-regulation and wellbeing workshops involving NHS staff at Whiston Hospital, alongside individual coaching and behaviour-change work.

When your organisation works with Clearer Mind, you deal directly with me about the audience, the aims of the sessions and their delivery. My NHS-related experience informs my practice but does not imply NHS endorsement of the programme.

My aim is straightforward: help people recognise what is happening earlier, develop useful ways to respond and leave with practical skills they can continue using.
