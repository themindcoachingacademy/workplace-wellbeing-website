On-site Wellbeing Clinics

Bring confidential employee support into the workplace.

Morning, afternoon and full-day wellbeing clinics for organisations within approximately 30 miles of St Helens.

Send an Enquiry

Standard clinic structure

  • 50-minute employee appointments
  • 10-minute buffer between appointments
  • 3 morning appointments
  • 4 afternoon appointments
  • 7 appointments on a full day

Clinic options

Morning

9:00am–12:00pm

Three confidential employee appointments.

Afternoon

12:30pm onward

Four appointments with the final session completed before 5:00pm.

Full Day

Seven appointments

Three morning and four afternoon appointments.

What the organisation provides

  • A private room where conversations cannot reasonably be overheard
  • A named administrative contact
  • Agreed employee access arrangements
  • A safeguarding/escalation contact where appropriate

Private self-booking

Employees self-book available clinic slots. The organisation can receive utilisation information without receiving confidential session content.

Unused appointments remain part of the booked clinic block because the practitioner time has been reserved.
